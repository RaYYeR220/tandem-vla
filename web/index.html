How it works

One sentence in. Four checks before anything moves.

Hear it, parse it, gate it, act on it. Every arrow below is a hard boundary — the model on the left of the gate never touches a motor, and the gate on the right of it never guesses.

01 · HEAR

Speechmatics streams a live transcript, or a scripted replay stands in when there's no key — labelled either way, never silently swapped.

02 · PARSE

A small instruction-tuned model, running on-device through OpenVINO, fills in six fields: what to place, whether to pour, what to refuse.

03 · GATE

Deterministic Python re-checks every step against the measured scene — reachability, gripper state, collisions — before a motor moves. No model in this box.

04 · ACT

The arms execute, handing objects across the shared zone because props start on the left and the setting sits on the right.
